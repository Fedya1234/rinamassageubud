How it works

In many small studios around Ubud one or two therapists work, so a couple books “together” and then one person reads on the sofa while the other is on the table. At Rina Massage five therapists work at the same time: two beds stand side by side in the same room, two therapists start together, and you finish together.

You do not have to choose the same treatment. One can take the strong Deep Healing Massage while the other has a gentle traditional Balinese massage or a facial — the timing simply matches. Prices are per person, straight from the normal menu; there is no surcharge for booking as a couple.

The room

One quiet room, two beds side by side, a traditional Balinese painting on the wall and the garden just outside the window. Towels, sarong and disposable underwear are provided, and the linen is changed for every guest.

Tell the therapists how strong you like the pressure — they adjust for each of you separately. Afterwards there is a place to sit; ask and we will make you a ginger tea.

Quietest hours: 9:00–11:00. Busiest: 11:00–13:00 and early evening.

Couples massage — questions

Can two people really be treated at the same time?

Yes — two beds, two therapists, one room, same start and finish. That is the main reason couples come back to us.

How much does it cost for two?

Per person from the normal menu: Balinese 60 min IDR 140,000, aromatherapy IDR 170,000, Deep Healing IDR 200,000, Hot Stone 90 min IDR 250,000. No couples surcharge.

Do we have to pick the same treatment?

No. Different treatments of the same length work best, and we will tell you if the timing does not match.

Can we come with friends?

Yes, up to five people in the same hour. Send a WhatsApp message a day ahead so all the therapists are free.

Is booking required?

For two people a walk-in usually works outside the busy hours. To be sure — especially around 11:00–13:00, in the early evening, or with three people and more — send one WhatsApp message: we normally confirm within 5–15 minutes and keep the therapists free. No deposit.
