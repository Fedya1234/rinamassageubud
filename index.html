A small studio with big experience

Rina Massage is a family-run massage studio on Jl. Raya Pengosekan Kelod, a short ride south of central Ubud. Our therapists trained and worked in Bali's well-known spas — here you get the same skilled hands, without the resort price tag.

Guests call our deep healing massage "the best massage in Ubud" — and at about half the price of the town centre. With five therapists, couples and groups can be treated at the same time.
