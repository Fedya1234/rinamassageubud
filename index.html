A small studio with big experience

Rina Massage is a family-run spa and massage studio on Jl. Raya Pengosekan Kelod, a short ride south of central Ubud. Our therapists trained and worked in Bali's well-known spas — here you get the same skilled hands without the resort price tag.

Guests call our deep healing massage “the best massage in Ubud”, and it costs about half of what you pay in the town centre. With five therapists, couples and small groups can be treated at the same time instead of waiting their turn.

Cash, QRIS and cards accepted · booking in one WhatsApp message · ginger tea on request after your treatment

Couples & group massage

Most small studios in Ubud have one or two therapists, so couples take turns. Here five therapists work at the same time — two people can lie side by side in the same room, and a group of friends or a family can all be treated in the same hour.

Pick the same treatment or different ones; the price is simply per person, straight from the menu. For three people or more, a message a day ahead helps us keep the therapists free for you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome, and for one person outside the busy hours it usually works straight away.

A WhatsApp message is simply easier: we normally confirm within 5–15 minutes and keep a therapist free for you — no deposit, no app. Around 11:00–13:00 and in the early evening, or if you are two or more people, we may already be full and have to ask walk-in guests to come back later.

Do you offer couples massage?

Yes. With five therapists a couple can be treated side by side at the same time, and small groups can book together. More about couples massage.

Which massage should I choose?

Traditional Balinese — light flowing pressure, the easiest first choice. Deep Healing — deeper work on tight muscles. Hot Stone — warm stones for a stiff back and shoulders. Reflexology — feet only, perfect after a day of walking.

See the full comparison.

How much does a massage cost?

30-minute treatments start at IDR 75,000. A one-hour traditional Balinese massage is IDR 140,000, Deep Healing IDR 200,000, and a 90-minute Hot Stone Massage IDR 250,000. No service charge is added.

What are your opening hours?

Every day, 9:00–21:00. If you are coming late in the evening, message us so the last slot is reserved for you.

How can I pay?

Cash in IDR, QRIS, and debit or credit cards including Visa and Mastercard.

Where exactly are you?

Jl. Raya Pengosekan Kelod, Mas, Ubud, Bali 80571 — roughly 10 minutes by scooter south of central Ubud. Open in Google Maps.
